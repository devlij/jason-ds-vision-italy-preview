IT-01-335 Roman columns of Brindisi, Brindisi
References:
  https://www.nomads-travel-guide.com/places/roman-columns-of-brindisi/
  https://www.apuliabiketours.com/antiche-strade-bicicletta-autoguidato-563991.html
Geometry anchors:
  1. One full Roman column with a Corinthian capital, standing on a pedestal.
  2. One broken stump beside it, not a second matching column.
  3. Both at the head of the wide ceremonial harbor stair, enclosed by town buildings: a pink palazzo facade and a stone balustrade. An urban square, not an open terrace.

IT-01-336 Ponte d'Augusto ruins, Narni
References:
  https://www.romanoimpero.com/2019/07/ponte-daugusto-narni-umbria.html
  https://www.umbriatourism.it/en/w/the-bridge-of-augustus-in-narni
Geometry anchors:
  1. Exactly one standing arch of large squared travertine blocks spanning the Nera gorge.
  2. Broken ruined piers and abutments on the wooded banks, and no second complete arch.
  3. Dense woodland around the gorge, with Narni's hill town above.

IT-01-337 Cosa ruins on Ansedonia headland, Ansedonia
References:
  https://www.romanoimpero.com/2017/04/cosa-toscana.html?m=1&hl=en
  https://robertharding.picfair.com/images/08757803-capitolium-ruins-of-main-temple-on-the-arx-roman-town-of-cosa-ansedonia
Geometry anchors:
  1. Massive polygonal-masonry city walls and a gate on the hillside.
  2. Capitolium on the arx reduced to a bare podium, with scattered column drums and foundation fragments.
  3. Low grassy ruins with pines and cypresses and the sea in the distance. No intact temple and no standing peripteral colonnade.

IT-01-339 Ponte di Ariccia and Palazzo Chigi, Ariccia
References:
  https://fabulous.huopenair.com/nl/ervaringen/castelli-romani
  https://www.artofit.org/ideas/ariccia
Geometry anchors:
  1. Bernini's rotunda: circular white-plastered drum, dark grey-blue dome and lantern, no campanili.
  2. Temple-front portico of three arched openings under a triangular pediment with the Chigi arms and the inscription DEIPARAE IN COELVM ASSVMPTAE.
  3. Two-tier brick Ponte di Ariccia viaduct, with Palazzo Chigi's long colonnaded front lining the Piazza di Corte beside the church.

IT-01-340 Rocca Monaldeschi above Lake Bolsena, Bolsena
References:
  https://www.visitlazio.com/eventi-lazio/festivita-del-corpus-domini-a-bolsena/
  https://destinazionevacanza.eurospin-viaggi.it/idee-vacanze/arte-e-cultura/guida-castelli-piu-belli-d-italia/
Geometry anchors:
  1. Brick quadrilateral fortress with four tall square crenellated towers and machicolated crowns at the corners.
  2. A courtyard block fronting a town square with a fountain.
  3. The fortress sits inside the medieval town above Lake Bolsena, with the lake visible behind and below the town. No round towers and no houses cascading down a cliff into the water.
