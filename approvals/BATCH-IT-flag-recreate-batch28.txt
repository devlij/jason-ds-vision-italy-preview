IT-01-330 Piazza Silvestri, Bevagna
References:
  https://www.pictorem.com/uploads/collection/I/IL10ENF8UHN/600_Marco-Brivio_UMBRIA_00102.jpg
  https://www.italyvacationspecialists.com/servimg/og/uploaded_files/location/2008-fac0d2bb07f76c2cb8eec49b4262f59a.jpg
Geometry anchors:
  1. Square stone campanile with pyramidal spire bonded into the LEFT side of the San Michele facade, immediately beside the large rose window.
  2. No detached bifora bell-gable between the church and the palazzo.
  3. Plain octagonal stone basin on a stepped plinth, simple central column, shallow upper bowl, no figural sculpture.

IT-01-331 Lungomare and Strait of Messina, Reggio Calabria
References:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Reggio_Calabria_-_Lungomare_Falcomat%C3%A0_-_2.jpg
  https://www.italia.it/es/abruzos/teramo/guia-historia-curiosidades
Geometry anchors:
  1. Black wrought-iron railings on short stone posts along the sea edge.
  2. Rows of globe-topped lamp posts, wide stone paving in a geometric grid, palms and benches inland, separate buildings across the road.
  3. Mount Etna across the Strait of Messina.

IT-01-332 Piombino promontory and fortress, Piombino
References:
  https://www.marina.difesa.it/cosa-facciamo/per-la-difesa-sicurezza/fari/Pagine/1426.aspx
  https://www.goodtrekking.it/percorsi/italia/trekking-toscana/alte-coste-rocciose-per-uno-dei-promontorio-piombino-piu-famosi-della-toscana-scopri-il-percorso-ad-anello-che-lo-circonda/
Geometry anchors:
  1. Slender stone Faro di Punta Falcone lighthouse with a lantern on the point.
  2. Open rocky maquis: low scrub, bare rock shoreline, small paved panoramic terrace, one or two low service buildings.
  3. No walls, no towers, no town — open promontory against the sea.

IT-01-333 Finale Ligure cliffs and Castel Gavone, Finale Ligure
References:
  https://mudifinale.com/sito-monumento/monumenti/castelli-e-fortificazioni/castel-gavone/
  https://visitfinaleligure.it/una-chef-in-cammino/
Geometry anchors:
  1. Round Torre dei Diamanti whose whole shaft is diamond-point ashlar, with a ragged crown of exposed red brick.
  2. Attached ruined palace walls and a stair.
  3. Wooded hillside above Finalborgo, town below, sea distant — not on the sea cliff.

IT-01-334 Cathedral village street, Sovana
References:
  https://www.visittuscany.com/it/attrazioni/duomo-di-sovana/
  https://borghipiubelliditalia.it/borgo/sovana/
Geometry anchors:
  1. Plain massive tuff ashlar church; the bare wall is pierced only by the white-marble portal (twisted colonnettes, crucifixion lintel, buttress-like pilasters).
  2. Small high windows and polygonal apse buttresses on the flank.
  3. No rose window and no campanile; the street leads to this sober stone mass.
