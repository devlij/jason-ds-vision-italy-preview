IT-01-351 Castle and cathedral ridge, Conversano
References:
  https://www.dizy.com/it/foto/1510746001
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Cattedrale_di_Santa_Maria_Assunta_(Conversano)
Geometry anchors:
  1. White-stone cathedral facade with a large rose window and two smaller oculi flanking it.
  2. Ornate carved portal with a Crucifixion in the tympanum.
  3. Low truncated octagonal campanile: short unfinished tower barely clearing the rooftops, flat unfinished top, one small open bell arch, no spire. The castle's cylindrical towers remain on the same ridge.

IT-01-352 Palazzo della Signoria, Jesi
References:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jesi,_Palazzo_della_Signoria.JPG
  https://pnrr.comune.jesi.an.it/web/guest/w/palazzo-della-signoria
Geometry anchors:
  1. Short unfinished brick tower with an open bell-arch belfry, no roof and no cross, rising just above the roofline.
  2. Round facade clock on the upper left.
  3. White-stone lion relief panel above the entrance portal. Facade windows are simple stone-framed rectangles, with no pediments.
