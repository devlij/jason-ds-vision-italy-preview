IT-01-341 Teramo Cathedral, Teramo
References:
  https://www.italia.it/it/abruzzo/teramo/musei/cattedrale-santa-maria-assunta
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teramo_Cathedral
Geometry anchors:
  1. Facade crowned with swallowtail Ghibelline merlons, and a small rose window set inside a pointed Gothic gable with flanking statuary niches above the single main portal.
  2. White-and-pink marble banding on the upper right block of the facade. No full-height arcade and no giant rose.
  3. Tall square campanile with a clock face and a pyramidal spire standing on the right of the facade, never on the left.

IT-01-343 Basilica della Santa Casa, Loreto
References:
  https://www.indaginiemisteri.it/la-santa-casa-di-loreto-tra-fede-e-mistero/
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basilica_della_Santa_Casa
Geometry anchors:
  1. A flat, uncurved white-marble screen: three portals with triangular pediments, statue niches, and a straight entablature. The dome stays visible behind the screen.
  2. Tall slender Vanvitelli campanile, open bell stage and small cupola, standing separate at the left of the facade.
  3. Long two-storey brick Apostolic Palace with an arcaded loggia running along the piazza at ground level. A palazzo, never a crenellated castle, never on an isolated ramp.

IT-01-345 La Nave di Cascella and pier, Pescara
References:
  https://www.travel365.it/pescara-cosa-vedere.htm
  https://www.musement.com/nl/pescara/
Geometry anchors:
  1. A low horizontal geometric marble ship of interlocking flat white-marble slabs, tilted discs, and a spiral element.
  2. The sculpture sits at water level inside a shallow rectangular basin on the lungomare.
  3. The pier is beside that basin. The work is horizontal and abstract-geometric, never a tall organic flame or sail standing on the sand.

IT-01-346 Rocca Roveresca, Mondavio
References:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rocca_Roveresca_-_Mondavio_8.JPG
  https://www.touringclub.it/eventi/caccia-ai-tesori-arancioni-di-mondavio-2025
Geometry anchors:
  1. The mastio is a massive battered brick tower with a sloping scarped base.
  2. A defining band of tall blind arches wraps the upper stage, under a plain low-pitched tiled roof, with no open crenellation.
  3. A small round turret on the enceinte has a low tiled cone roof. The fortress is set in the town, not a ring of matching round keeps.

IT-01-347 Torre del Passero skyline, Recanati
References:
  https://marcheoutdoor.it/PuntiDiInteresse/Dettagli/mopa_srl
  https://www.dizy.com/it/foto/1507056003
Geometry anchors:
  1. A civic brick tower: a plain square brick shaft.
  2. The shaft rises to a machicolated corbelled cornice capped by a low octagonal top stage. No pointed spire.
  3. No church is attached. The tower stands with the old civic walls in the historic center, and is never rendered as a church campanile.
